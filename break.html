EDER POETRY

More Than a Lifesaver

12th January 2026

A rare and radiant resonance takes hold,
When silver tongues strike chords of solid gold.
No longer drifting, disparate and lone,
Two seeds in sync have finally been sown.

A silent pact, a pulse within the chest,
Where weary ambition finds its place to rest.
No friction flares to fray the common thread;
By shared intent, the hungry flame is fed.

The boardroom’s fire and the hearth’s soft glow,
Are fueled by winds that in one direction blow.
A builder’s grip, a lover’s light embrace,
Reflected in the mirror of a kindred face.

So rare the find, this fierce and fluid bond,
Beyond the pale, and deeper than the pond.
A world of two, a fortress and a flight,
A dual sun that burns against the night.

EDERPOETRY / SELECTED WORK

The Silent Room

10th May 2026

The silent room still holds your faded grace,
As shadow-ghosts dance softly on the wall.
I reach across to touch your absent face,
And listen for a voice that will not call.

You tore the fragile fibers of my trust,
And left me bleeding in the cold alone.
Our golden promises have turned to dust,
My beating heart transformed to heavy stone.

I tear apart the memories of your smile,
And mourn the love I thought was built to stay.
I sat and waited for a little while,
Hoping you’d return and wash the dark away.

Now tears fall silent in the midnight air,
A bitter grief no soothing words can mend.
I’m trapped inside this echo of despair,
Still loving you, though you became the end.

EDERPOETRY / SELECTED WORK

A Missing Memory

20th August 2024

I trace a line upon an empty page,
A silhouette without a name or face.
I fight a quiet, hollow kind of rage,
And search for roots inside a blank embrace.

They speak of men who taught them how to stand,
Who held them close and guided them along.
I never felt the comfort of his hand,
Nor heard the gentle cadence of his song.

A piece of me is buried out of sight,
A ghost who walks inside my very vein.
I whisper to the stillness of the night,
And ask the dark to justify the pain.

So here I linger, whole yet incomplete,
A legacy carved out of mystery.
I walk this road on unsteady feet,
A child born from a missing memory.

EDERPOETRY / SELECTED WORK

Breaking Poverty’s Chains

18th September 2026

I bore the heavy chains of empty hands,
And felt the bitter frost of endless night.
When umaskini ruled my native lands,
I begged the heavens for a drop of light,

While hunger burned me like a raging brand.
That merciless adui stole my youth,
And wrapped my dreams in rags of grey despair.
I learned the harsh and cold, unyielding truth—

When nia struggles under heavy care,
The path of life becomes a jagged tooth.
I watched my people bleed beneath its reign,
Where mafanikio seemed a distant shore.

Yet deep within the quiet of my pain,
I swore I would not suffer anymore,
And broke the iron links of sorrow's chain.
Now standing tall above the battle line,

With ushindi glowing in my chest,
I leave the shadows of that dark design.
No longer bound, no longer dispossessed,
The dawn of strength and freedom now is mine.

EDER POETRY / RESTLESS HOPE · ABAB

The Last Matatu Home

17th October 2025 · 23:41 EAT

The city folded slowly under amber light,
While tired shoes kept time along the road.
A final matatu carried through the night,
With half-made dreams still heavy in their load.

At every stop, another face would rise,
Some chasing rent, some chasing sleep instead.
The windows held a thousand private skies,
And streetlights stitched their gold above each head.

I watched the roadside shops become a blur,
And felt tomorrow knocking without sound.
Some nights the heart has nowhere else to stir,
So motion becomes the safest ground.

When home appeared beyond the final bend,
The night had lost the power to pretend.
I stepped out lighter than when the journey began,
Not healed, perhaps, but ready to begin again.

One road can carry more than tired feet,
And hope can hide inside a city street,
Waiting for dawn to make the ending sweet.

EDER POETRY / PRESSURE AND DETERMINATION · AABB

Coffee Before the Deadline

29th November 2025 · 02:17 EAT

The screen stayed bright while all the rooms were dark,
A blinking cursor throwing sparks.
The clock moved on with ruthless grace,
While unfinished work refused its place.

A cold cup waited beside the chair,
Proof that another long night had been there.
The easy choice was sleep and let it slide,
But something stubborn stayed awake inside.

One line worked, then another broke,
Progress arrived like a private joke.
Still, every error taught the hand,
And every small fix helped things stand.

By morning the page was no longer bare,
The tired eyes could finally stare.
The deadline had not become kind or fair,
But effort had already carried its share.

Not every victory makes a sound,
Some simply leave a quiet glow around,
And prove the night was worth being found.

EDER POETRY / LONELINESS AND OBSERVATION · ABBA

A Seat by the Window

8th January 2026 · 18:26 EAT

A quiet seat beside the glass,
Held one more evening moving past.
The world outside refused to slow,
While thoughts inside moved soft and low.

Two strangers laughed across the aisle,
Then disappeared beyond a mile.
A vendor counted coins with care,
And carried ordinary life through air.

There was no tragedy to name,
No thunder, no dramatic flame.
Just one small ache that would not speak,
And one long week that felt too weak.

Yet somewhere between dusk and rain,
The heart made room for joy again.
A song arrived, a child smiled wide,
And loneliness sat down beside the tide.

Some healing enters without a door,
Not as a cure, but something more,
A reason to keep looking outside once more.

EDER POETRY / REGRET AND RESTRAINT · ABCB

Things I Never Sent

14th February 2026 · 21:08 EAT

There were words beneath the thumb,
Waiting underneath the light.
Some belonged to yesterday,
Some would only reopen night.

A name appeared, then disappeared,
The message stayed unsent.
Sometimes silence costs much less
Than saying what was meant.

Old promises can sound sincere
When memory softens pain.
But distance teaches different truths
That closeness hides again.

So the screen went dark at last,
The room returned to still.
Not every feeling needs an audience,
Not every wound needs a will.

The words remained, but lost their claim,
No longer calling out a name,
And peace arrived without a message to explain.

Rain on the Campus Walk

27th March 2026 · 16:52 EAT

Rain wrote circles on the ground,
Umbrellas gathered all around,
Footsteps hurried past the gate,
While evening learned to wait.

A notebook darkened at the edge,

For a little while, the storm was proof

EDER POETRY / HIDDEN EXHAUSTION · AABB

The Price of Looking Fine

3rd May 2026 · 00:36 EAT

A clean shirt can hide a tired face,
A steady walk can hide the race.
A practiced laugh can fill the room,
While private thoughts still fear the gloom.

The mirror knows what crowds ignore,
The pauses between the closing door.
It sees the shoulders carrying weight,
And eyes that learned to negotiate.

No medal waits for staying strong,
No one can count the nights gone wrong.
Sometimes the bravest thing to do
Is admit the day was hard to move through.

Then morning comes without applause,
Still asking life to state its cause.
The face is washed, the shoes are tied,
And courage walks along beside.

Strength is not a flawless disguise,
It is making room for honest eyes,
And letting tired hearts survive.

EDER POETRY / BELONGING AND RELEASE · ABAB

Football Under Floodlights

18th June 2026 · 19:23 EAT

The floodlights cut the evening blue,
The first touch rolled across the grass.
A crowded sideline shouted through,
And every minute seemed to pass.

A missed chance hurt, then laughter came,
A teammate raised a tired hand.
For ninety minutes there was one shared game,
One moving pulse across the land.

No lecture asked who people were,
No past could enter through the line.
The whistle made the noise occur,
And made the ordinary feel divine.

When final footsteps left the field,
The silence felt both strange and right.
Some battles only need to yield,
Then leave a little room for light.

The score will fade, the evening too,
But belonging stays when people do,
And shared joy makes the dark feel new.

EDER POETRY / GROWTH AND SELF-DOUBT · ABBA

A Better Version Tomorrow

31st July 2026 · 05:12 EAT

The alarm arrived before the sun,
A new day asking what was done.
The room was still, the streets asleep,
While promises were hard to keep.

A list of goals lay on the floor,
Some crossed out, some waiting more.
Progress looked smaller than the plan,
Yet small steps still became the span.

There was a time when every flaw
Felt like a permanent old law.
Now mistakes could teach instead,
And failure could be moved ahead.

No perfect self appeared that day,
Just one more reason not to stay
Inside the fear of getting less
When growth arrives through awkwardness.

Tomorrow does not need a crown,
It only needs one step from ground,
And one more chance to turn around.

The Message at 3:06

20th August 2026 · 03:06 EAT

A phone lit up beside the bed,
Three small numbers broke the dark.
No miracle had crossed the room,
Just one message leaving a spark.

The words were simple, almost plain,
Yet somehow changed the air.
A reminder that somewhere beyond
There was still somebody who cared.

Its silence hard to bear.

The same old world remained.
Still, hope can start with very little,
A door where none was named.

Some nights survive on fragile light,

EDER POETRY / ACCEPTANCE AND RENEWAL · AABC

Before This Day Ends

21st September 2026 · 22:48 EAT

The day is almost out of view,
The room has turned a darker blue,
And unfinished thoughts still wait,
But not every loose end is fate.

Some plans worked and some went wrong,
Some doubts stayed longer than too long.
A few old fears returned to speak,
Then lost their volume by the week.

There is no need to fix it all,
No need to make the past stand tall.
Some chapters end without a sign,
And some beginnings take their time.

So let the unfinished pieces stay,
They do not own another day.
Tomorrow can arrive without a vow,
Just bringing another chance somehow.

The night is not a final door,
It is a pause before the next shore,
And there is still more life to write.
