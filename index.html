EDERPOETRY / FEATURED

Breaking Poverty’s Chains

18TH SEP 2026

EP

A poem about struggle, resilience, ambition and the decision to rise beyond poverty.

Heavy chains of empty hands remain,
As bitter frost consumes the endless night.
Where umaskini leaves a lasting stain,
The faintest dreams still reach for drops of light.

While hunger burns beneath a ruthless brand,
That merciless adui steals away youth.
Dreams wrapped in rags across the barren land,
Reveal the harsh and cold, unyielding truth.

When nia struggles under heavy care,
The path of life becomes a jagged road.
Across the earth, worn faces bear despair,
While mafanikio waits beyond the load.

Yet deep within the ruins of the pain,
A spark survives beneath the weight of scars.
The iron links of sorrow break like rain,
And courage rises past the fallen bars.

With ushindi glowing through the darkest night,
The shadows fade beneath a rising sun.
No longer bound by poverty's cruel might,
The dawn of strength declares that chains are done.
